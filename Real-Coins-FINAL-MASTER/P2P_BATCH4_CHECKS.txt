P2P Batch 4 checks/fixes:
- BUY/SELL ads show min/max order limits in ETB.
- Buyer/seller enters an ETB order amount before starting a trade.
- 499 ETB is rejected for a 500 ETB minimum; 1001 ETB is rejected for a 1000 ETB maximum.
- Order amount is converted to REAL using the ad price per RC.
- SELL ads lock only the REAL amount posted in that specific ad, never the user wallet balance.
- SELL ad creation is rejected when posted REAL exceeds available REAL.
- BUY ad creation does not require REAL balance/deposit.
- BUY ad locks the counterparty seller's REAL only when a trade is actually started, and only for that trade amount.
- Partial orders reduce the ad's remaining REAL amount and remaining ETB capacity.
- Completing a trade releases only that order's escrow and leaves the remaining ad available.
- Expiring a BUY trade refunds only that trade's seller lock and restores the ad amount.
