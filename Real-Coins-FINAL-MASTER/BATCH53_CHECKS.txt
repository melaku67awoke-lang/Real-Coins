Batch 53 — Spin Wheel / 24-hour reward timer

Implemented:
- One free spin every 24 hours.
- After the free spin is used, additional spins cost exactly 10 RC.
- Paid spins deduct only from available REAL balance (locked REAL cannot be spent).
- Spin outcomes above 10 RC have a configured probability of exactly 20% (20 RC = 15%, 50 RC = 5%).
- Free-spin timestamp is persisted in Room with migration 12 -> 13.
- The 24-hour countdown updates every second and survives app restarts because the timestamp is persisted.
- The daily reward/free-spin uses the same 24-hour timer.
- Daily reward activity requirements remain enforced for the free spin.
- Spin payouts and paid-spin charges are written to the local immutable transaction ledger.
- Fixed the previous callback so a completed wheel spin is not accidentally processed a second time.

Build note:
- Full Android Gradle build was not run because the project does not contain the Gradle wrapper JAR/script in this ZIP.
