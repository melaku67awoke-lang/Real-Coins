Batch 51 - User Levels, Daily Rewards, Deposit Bonus Safety

Implemented:
- Starter: 0 USD total confirmed deposits -> 10 RC daily
- Silver: 100 USD -> 30 RC daily
- Bronze: 200 USD -> 50 RC daily
- Gold: 300 USD -> 75 RC daily
- Platinum: 400 USD -> 100 RC daily
- Diamond: 500 USD -> 150 RC daily
- KING: 1000 USD -> 300 RC daily
- Level uses total confirmed deposit volume in USD, recorded at deposit time.
- Daily reward amount is server-side/local logic derived from level; caller-supplied reward is ignored.
- After each 7 claimed reward days, the next reward cycle requires one completed P2P buy and one completed P2P sell with different counterparties.
- Deposit withdrawal/sell balance does not reset the level because level is based on confirmed deposit volume.
- Deposit screen now accepts USD, shows today's RC conversion and 10% bonus calculation, and enforces $25 minimum.
- Deposit approval awards the 10% bonus as locked promotional RC, excluded from withdrawable/spendable REAL balance, preventing withdraw/redeposit bonus abuse.
- Minimum withdrawal remains $50 USD.
- User profile/home shows current level, daily reward, total deposit volume, next level, and progress.
- Room migration 10->11 adds bonus balance and deposit USD/bonus fields.
